01 — Notes

A notebook that renders math and remembers where you drew the arrow.

Rich text with fullscreen focus mode, inline and block KaTeX equations built from a click-to-insert symbol palette (no LaTeX memorized), and photos you can draw, highlight, or arrow directly on top of.

  • Ctrl/Cmd+. drops everything else and just leaves the page
  • Freehand pen, highlighter, and arrow annotation on any inserted image
  • Mindmaps, spreadsheet+chart blocks, and lecture audio recording built in

02 — Calendar

Every calendar you're forced to have, layered into one.

Personal Google account, school Google account, Google Classroom, any number of ICS feeds your school publishes, your real (even rotating) class schedule, and your own daily routine — each in its own color, each toggleable.

  • Multi-account Google OAuth with a Calendar-only fallback for locked-down school admins
  • Handles genuine rotating day schedules (1A–8A style cycles), not just Mon–Fri
  • Push planner items back out to whichever calendar you choose

03 — Gemini study engine

Autocomplete that's read your notes, not the whole internet.

Ghost-text suggestions while you type, built only from the paragraph you're actually writing. Turn any tagged set of notes into a study guide or a practice exam — on demand, never automatically, never touching your calendar or grades.

  • Tab to accept a suggestion, keep typing to ignore it
  • Works offline / without a key — it just quietly doesn't fire
  • Your own free API key from Google AI Studio, nothing shared

04 — Campus map

Know your next class before the bell rings.

Reads your live schedule, works out what's happening now or next, and highlights exactly which room — with a plain-language direction, not a guess.

  • Ships with an example floor plan; point it at your own school's rooms
  • Updates automatically as your day-code / rotation changes
